Summary

The Starter package is built for freelancers, solo creatives, and personal brands who need a clean, professional online presence that actually represents the quality of their work. If you're just getting started or you've been operating without a proper site for too long, this is where that changes.

What's Included

Up to 4 pages

Mobile responsive across all devices

Contact form setup

Basic SEO — title tags, meta descriptions, image optimisation, clean URLs

1 round of revisions

Full handover with site walkthrough

The Process

The Studio package runs through five phases — Discovery, Design Direction, Full Design, Build, and Launch — and is completed within 2 to 3 weeks from the point your brief and assets are confirmed.
